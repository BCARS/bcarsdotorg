## W3ZC Special Event Station — World's Largest Coffee Pot & USA 250th Anniversary

<img width="400" alt="The World's Largest Coffee Pot in Bedford, PA" src="/images/coffeepot.jpg" />

**October 3, 2026 — 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (EDT) | 1200Z–2359Z**

BCARS will be operating special event station **W3ZC** from the World's Largest Coffee Pot in Bedford, PA to celebrate the iconic landmark and the USA's 250th Anniversary. Contacts count towards the ARRL **WAS250** (Worked All States 250) award.

### Frequencies

| Band | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| 40 Meters | 7.215 MHz |
| 20 Meters | 14.255 MHz |
| 15 Meters | 21.225 MHz |

Mode: SSB

### QSL / Certificate

To receive a QSL card, send a self-addressed stamped envelope (SASE) to:

> Bedford County Amateur Radio Society
> 120 W. Watson Street
> Bedford, PA 15522

### About the Coffee Pot

The World's Largest Coffee Pot is a Bedford County landmark — a 18-foot tall steel coffee pot originally built in 1927 as a roadside attraction. It sits along the Lincoln Highway (US Route 30) in Bedford, PA.

### About BCARS

BCARS (Bedford County Amateur Radio Society) is an ARRL Special Service Club. Visit [bcars.org](https://bcars.org) for more information.
